🇬🇧 INGLESE

BE - PAST SIMPLE

Impara come usare il verbo to be al passato.

📖 Forma affermativa

Nel Past Simple il verbo to be diventa was oppure were.

Italiano Inglese
Io ero I was
Tu eri You were
Lui era He was
Lei era She was
Esso era It was
Noi eravamo We were
Voi eravate You were
Loro erano They were

❌ Forma negativa

Per fare la forma negativa si aggiunge not.

Affermativa Negativa
I was I was not (wasn't)
You were You were not (weren't)
He was He wasn't
She was She wasn't
It was It wasn't
We were We weren't
You were You weren't
They were They weren't

❓ Forma interrogativa

Nella domanda il verbo va prima del soggetto.

Italiano Inglese
Ero io? Was I?
Eri tu? Were you?
Era lui? Was he?
Era lei? Was she?
Era? Was it?
Eravamo noi? Were we?
Eravate voi? Were you?
Erano loro? Were they?

✏️ Esempi

I was at school yesterday.
Ieri ero a scuola.

She was happy.
Lei era felice.

They were tired.
Loro erano stanchi.

He wasn't at home.
Lui non era a casa.

Were you at the cinema?
Eri al cinema?

💡 Da ricordare

  • ✅ I / He / She / It → was
  • ✅ You / We / They → were
  • ✅ Negativa = was not / were not
  • ✅ Domanda = Was...? / Were...?
