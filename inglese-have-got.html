🇬🇧 INGLESE

HAVE GOT

Impara come usare have got per esprimere il possesso.

📖 Forma affermativa

Have got significa avere.

Italiano Inglese
Io hoI have got (I've got)
Tu haiYou have got (You've got)
Lui haHe has got (He's got)
Lei haShe has got (She's got)
Esso haIt has got (It's got)
Noi abbiamoWe have got (We've got)
Voi aveteYou have got (You've got)
Loro hannoThey have got (They've got)

Esempi

  • I have got a dog.
  • She has got blue eyes.
  • They have got two brothers.

❌ Forma negativa

Italiano Inglese
Io non hoI haven't got
Tu non haiYou haven't got
Lui non haHe hasn't got
Lei non haShe hasn't got
Esso non haIt hasn't got
Noi non abbiamoWe haven't got
Voi non aveteYou haven't got
Loro non hannoThey haven't got

Esempi

  • I haven't got a bike.
  • He hasn't got a sister.

❓ Forma interrogativa

Inglese Italiano

Has she got...?Ha lei...?
Has it got...?Ha...?
Have we got...?Abbiamo...?
Have you got...?Avete...?
Have they got...?Hanno...?

Esempi

  • Have you got a pen?
  • Has she got a cat?

💬 Risposte brevi

Positive Negative
Yes, I have. No, I haven't.
Yes, you have. No, you haven't.
Yes, he has. No, he hasn't.
Yes, she has. No, she hasn't.
Yes, it has. No, it hasn't.
Yes, we have. No, we haven't.
Yes, they have. No, they haven't.

⭐ Da ricordare

  • Have got significa avere.
  • Con he, she, it si usa has got.
  • Con I, you, we, they si usa have got.
  • Nelle domande si mette Have o Has all'inizio.
  • Nella forma negativa si usa haven't got oppure hasn't got.
